\section{Preliminaries}

In this section, we introduce the notation and terminology required for our analysis.

\paragraph{Online Model.}
Following the framework of Cohen-Addad et al.~\cite{cohen2022online}, we consider an online variant of the consensus clustering problem.
Vertices arrive sequentially over time.
Let $V^t \subseteq V$ denote the set of vertices that have arrived by time step $t$.

The collection of input clusterings $\m{I} = \{\m{C}_1, \ldots, \m{C}_m\}$ is defined on the full vertex set $V$, but is not fully revealed initially.
At time step $t$, for each input clustering $\m{C}_i \in \m{I}$, the algorithm is only given its restriction to the arrived vertices,
\[
    \m{C}_i^t = \m{C}_i[V^t].
\]
That is, $\m{C}_i^t$ consists of the clusters obtained by intersecting each cluster of $\m{C}_i$ with $V^t$.

\paragraph{Objective of the \(\occ\) problem.}
At every time step \(t\), the algorithm must maintain a clustering \(S_t\) of the currently arrived vertices \(V^t\). For two clusterings \(\m{A}\) and \(\m{B}\) of \(V^t\), we write
\[
    \dist_t(\m{A},\m{B})
    :=
    \dist_{V^t}(\m{A},\m{B})
\]
to denote their pairwise disagreement distance restricted to the vertex set \(V^t\).

For any clustering \(\m{C}\) of \(V^t\), we define its online consensus objective at time \(t\) as
\[
    \obj_t(\m{C})
    :=
    \sum_{i=1}^m
    \dist_t(\m{C}_i^t,\m{C}).
\]
Let
\[
    \OPT_t
    \in
    \arg\min_{\m{C}\text{ clustering of }V^t}
    \obj_t(\m{C})
\]
denote an optimal offline consensus clustering of the arrived vertices at time \(t\). Thus,
\[
    \obj_t(\OPT_t)
    =
    \min_{\m{C}\text{ clustering of }V^t}
    \obj_t(\m{C}).
\]

An online algorithm is said to be \(\alpha\)-competitive if, for every time step \(t\),
\[
    \obj_t(S_t)
    \le
    \alpha \cdot \obj_t(\OPT_t).
\]
